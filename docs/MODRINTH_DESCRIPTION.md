# Custom Apples

A Forge progression mod where **every tool, food, and block is an apple**. Unlock recipes in the vanilla recipe book, spread orange apple juice from leaves, shrink mobs with the Apple Sword, and reach the **Dragon Apple** Big Tree for the real **APPLE**.

---

## Inspired by xNestorio

Inspired by **[xNestorio](https://www.youtube.com/@xNestorio)** and his custom-apple progression.

**Watch the original:** [Minecraft, But Apples Are Stronger Than Netherite...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NlLT-nubV4)

[![xNestorio custom apples video thumbnail linking to YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/7NlLT-nubV4/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NlLT-nubV4)

> Fan recreation — not an official xNestorio project. Credit to xNestorio for the idea and progression style.

---

## Highlights

- **40+ custom items** — food, tools, armor, blocks, throwable apples
- **Recipe book progression** — unlock as you craft (no custom GUI)
- **Apple juice fluid** — milk from leaves; spreads like water; trees grow where juice spreads
- **Worm pet** — from Dirt Apple; name GUI + crawl animation
- **Apple Bell** — apple-textured villagers with apple trades
- **Super Apple Pickaxe** — 3×3 mining
- **Dragon Apple** — giant Big Tree + exterior reward chest
- **APPLE** — 3D photoscan item (eating it kicks you from the world)
